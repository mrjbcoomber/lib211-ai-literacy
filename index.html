PART 2 · TRAINING VERSUS USING AI

How does an AI model learn?

Before a language model can answer questions, it goes through training. It processes many examples and adjusts numerical values called parameters to learn patterns and relationships.

PART 3 · TEXT BECOMES TOKENS

How does the model read your question?

The text is broken into smaller pieces called tokens. Tokens can be whole words, parts of words, or punctuation. Each token is converted to a numerical ID, then represented mathematically.

Why is the sky blue?

The model processes numerical representations of text, not letters on a screen in the way you read them.

PART 6 · ACTIVATE YOUR THINKING

What does this mean for learning?

AI can generate useful and sophisticated language. But polished writing alone doesn't show that every claim is true—or that a student submitting it understands the subject.

Think about this:

If ChatGPT writes your essay, what skills might you miss practicing? How could AI instead help you learn?
