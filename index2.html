How Do LLMs Learn?

From training data to a useful AI assistant — explore five stages, then test what you understand.

About 15–20 minutes · No account needed · Educational demonstrations

Choose a guided sequence or open individual stages. Both include the same explanations and interactive practice.

What should you take away?

Training helps models learn complex patterns and capabilities, but the process also reflects choices about data, feedback, and evaluation. When a model produces a response, you still need to judge whether it is accurate, relevant, and useful for your own learning.
