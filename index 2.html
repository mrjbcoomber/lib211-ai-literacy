PART 4 · CONNECT TO REAL LANGUAGE MODELS

Our toy model vs. ChatGPT

Our model only tracks four possible next words in one sentence. ChatGPT and Claude work on a vastly larger scale.

Our toy model

Four word choices, hand-built scores, one short sentence

Real LLM training

Large datasets, many layers, parameters adjusted through optimization

Real LLM responses

Prompt becomes tokens; model uses learned parameters to generate output tokens

Training is different from answering

When you ask a trained model a question, it normally uses the parameters learned during training. Your prompt can change the answer without retraining the model.

PART 5 · ACTIVATE YOUR THINKING

What does this mean for your learning?

A language model can produce impressive, useful writing. But a polished response doesn't automatically prove that every claim is correct—or that a student who submits it has learned the material.

Think about this:

If AI writes your assignment for you, what opportunities to practice thinking, researching, or writing might you miss?
